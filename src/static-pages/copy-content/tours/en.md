# CV Show

## identity / Who I am
Hi, I am Vladimir Matiasevich, a Lead R&D Engineer. I have spent more than fifteen years building software products, media systems, and hardware. Today I combine that experience into controlled automation loops.

## problem / The problem I solve
I work on processes that require a sequence of verifiable actions. A system needs to observe its current state, choose the next action, execute it, and verify the result. I design that loop end to end, from the interface and backend to browser automation or a physical device.

## agent-portal / Agent Portal
Agent Portal is my working environment for AI-assisted engineering. Its MCP tools assemble project context, build a code graph, coordinate execution, and operate the browser and terminal. Transitions, completion criteria, and result checks are part of the process.

## symbiote / The Symbiote line
Symbiote develops the same idea as a product line. Workspace defines a task-adaptive environment, UI supplies reusable components, and Engine executes process graphs. This is my current R&D direction for systems assembled around a concrete job.

## museum-capture / Museum digitization
Another part of my work is high-detail 3D capture. I developed technology and equipment for OBJET.art and ArtClub Digital Heritage, used at the State Hermitage Museum, Kunstkamera, other museums, and private collections. Software, lighting, mechanics, and field operation formed one system.

## method / How I work
I usually begin by studying the domain and observing the real process. Then I lock the architecture, build a prototype, and test it under working conditions. Review, context documentation, and explicit ownership help carry an experiment into operation.

## pulse / A public decision record
The Pulse section contains release notes, experiments, and engineering decisions from these projects. Each entry connects a product state with the reasons, constraints, and outcome of a concrete iteration.

## offer / What I offer
I am open to new opportunities and work remotely. I can join as a hands-on lead or senior individual contributor; for broader work I can integrate the RND-PRO team. Continue through the projects here, or contact me through Telegram and LinkedIn.
