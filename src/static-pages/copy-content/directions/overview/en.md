## The work I do

As a Product Engineer, I build automation systems for processes that connect software services, data, and devices. Some projects bring separate components together into a dependable product; others assign repeatable work to agents and verify the result.

## Areas of work

- **[Systems engineering and integration](systems-engineering/)** — designing a product as a whole: its interface, APIs, services, data stores, queues, and device connections.
- **[Applied AI and agent systems](applied-ai/)** — tools and workflows that give models and agents the context, tasks, and resources they need.
- **[Process automation](process-automation/)** — browser and mobile operations with condition checks, saved state, and controlled retries.
