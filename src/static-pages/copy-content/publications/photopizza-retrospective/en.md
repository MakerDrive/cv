I invented PhotoPizza inside MEGAVISOR as a motorized turntable for affordable photo-360 product capture. Within MEGAVISOR I defined the capture logic and equipment requirements; a contracted specialist implemented the first Arduino controller software. After MEGAVISOR, I began developing the control software myself in JavaScript and Espruino.

The public open-source project contains laser-cut turntable drawings, control-unit hardware, firmware, and operator documentation. Its controllers support IR, Wi-Fi/browser, and Android operation for settings such as frame count, speed, pause, delay, direction, calibration, and continuous rotation.

PhotoPizza supports photography and video as well as photogrammetric 3D scanning. The documented workflow rotates the object through configured capture positions and exposes the pause and delay needed around each frame. The equipment was used in later scanning work, including the technical line that led to ComplexScan and AUTOBOX.
