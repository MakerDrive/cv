I developed AUTOBOX's custom modular lighting as part of the capture technology for the cultural-heritage platform now operating as OBJET.art. The lights supported wireless control, active cooling, sensors, and cross-polarization for photogrammetric work with difficult museum materials.

The Hermitage netsuke were a demanding validation set: carved relief, cord holes, wood, ivory, horn, glossy surfaces, and translucent inserts placed geometry, texture, color, and material response in the same capture task. I personally scanned the collection during production validation.

The same equipment line was used for Benin bronze objects at the Kunstkamera. The museums provided collections and field settings; AUTOBOX technology and equipment belonged to the OBJET.art / ArtClub Digital Heritage product line.
