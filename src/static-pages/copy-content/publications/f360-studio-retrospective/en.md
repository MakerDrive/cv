I founded and ran F360 as a high-precision 3D-scanning studio after the museum work with AUTOBOX. From 2021 to 2022, the studio applied that capture discipline to commercial digitization of products and other physical objects.

The workflow linked the physical setup to the complete production chain: capture planning, controlled lighting, source-image quality checks, photogrammetry, geometry and texture processing, and presentation of finished models. Public examples remain on the F360 YouTube channel and in the F360 Studio Sketchfab portfolio.

F360 operated alongside the continuing PhotoPizza and ComplexScan lines as a studio and professional-experience project that I founded.
