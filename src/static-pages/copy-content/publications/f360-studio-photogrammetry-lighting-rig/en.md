At F360, controlled lighting and source-image quality were part of the photogrammetry workflow from the start. I linked capture planning and the physical studio setup to geometry reconstruction, texture processing, and presentation of the finished model.

The studio carried capture discipline from the earlier museum-scanning work into commercial digitization of products and other physical objects. Public process examples remain on the F360 YouTube channel, while finished models are available in the F360 Studio Sketchfab portfolio.
