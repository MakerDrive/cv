I developed this confidential lifecycle-messaging platform as a commercial R&D project. Its public scope is consent-based customer communication: opt-in SMS scenarios, campaign orchestration, audience segmentation, analytics, role-based workflows, and operational automation.

The technology profile includes JavaScript and Node.js API services, PostgreSQL, WebSocket runtime synchronization, RabbitMQ-compatible intake, and Swagger documentation. Delivery operations include PM2 and nginx, SSH/WebSocket tunnels, serial/COM control of GSM modem pools, AT commands, SMS inbox handling, and USSD operations.

My contribution was to turn communication, audience, hardware-runtime, and operations requirements into product architecture, data flows, modem-side execution, server infrastructure, admin interfaces, and repeatable operational tools. Client identity and implementation-specific details remain outside the public description.
