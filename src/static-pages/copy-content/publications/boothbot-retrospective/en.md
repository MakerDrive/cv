I built BoothBot as a commercial automation project for repeatable catalog photography of reflective wine bottles at a customer's warehouse. The equipment combined a compact booth, controlled light panels, a motorized camera, capture presets, and an automated photo-processing workflow.

The operator surface exposed the controls needed to run the capture sequence, while the equipment kept lighting and camera positions repeatable. Staff without studio experience could produce consistent catalog images on the warehouse floor.

BoothBot became a distinct branch of my equipment and control-software R&D. It applied methods developed around 360-degree media and scanning to an ordinary catalog process with a difficult subject and a real warehouse operating environment.
