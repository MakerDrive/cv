I invented ComplexScan as a commercial hardware product after the open-source PhotoPizza line. It used a transparent rotating disc made from tempered glass, stable mechanics, and controlled light around and below the object for photogrammetric 3D scanning, 360-degree capture, and product photography.

I designed the physical system and capture method together. The transparent platform and lighting configuration improved masks, source textures, and geometry before the material entered 3D processing, reducing retouching at the capture stage.

My role covered concept and R&D, mechanical design, handmade prototypes, testing, production setup, final assembly, packaging, export documentation, and early deliveries to customers in several countries. Specialist contractors fabricated parts such as laser-cut metal and acrylic; I coordinated that work and completed assembly and testing. The resulting R&D later informed the AUTOBOX museum-scanning line.
