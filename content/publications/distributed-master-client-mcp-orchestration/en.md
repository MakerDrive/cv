Agent Portal runs in standalone, client, and master modes. A standalone node owns its local MCP servers. A client connects to a master over WebSocket and shares its local tools; the master combines them with its own servers behind one portal.

On each host, IDE windows connect through stdio proxies to a detached singleton backend. The smart gateway exposes `discover_tools` to find public tools, `call_tool` to validate and dispatch a selected call, and `get_portal_status` to report public servers and runtime health. Agent Pool handles chat orchestration inside the portal. If a WebSocket connection drops, the client reports the loss and attempts a bounded reconnect.
