## What changed

Four Pulse articles described the same practice areas from a single moment in time. They are now permanent sections of the portfolio, because a direction is not a publication: it is the accumulated answer to "what do you solve", with the project periods, the personal contribution, and the line between what runs and what is still a plan.

## Where the texts went

- **Systems Engineering and Integration** — [read the direction](../directions/systems-engineering/). Source: the article on full-stack and systems integration.
- **Applied AI Infrastructure and Agent Delegation** — [read the direction](../directions/applied-ai/). Sources: the articles on AI deployment control and on delegation to verified action.
- **Process Automation and Verification** — [read the direction](../directions/process-automation/). Source: the article on web form automation with a verified request route.

## What happens to the old addresses

The old Pulse addresses keep working. Each of them now leads to the permanent section that replaced it, is marked `noindex, follow`, and points its canonical tag at that section, so the article text is not duplicated as a competing page.

## Why this is dated today

The date on this note is the date the portfolio was reorganised, not the date the practice started. The projects keep their own periods, and each direction page states when its source text was published.
