I co-founded MEGAVISOR and served as technical director, leading the capture-side R&D around its cloud publishing product. I developed the photo-360 capture technology and equipment for products ranging from phones to motorcycles, including lighting and the field production process. I wrote the controller-software specification; a contracted specialist implemented the first Arduino version.

MEGAVISOR's capture studio was called Gate9. I organized photo-360 sequence and spherical 3D panorama shoots in customer warehouses and stores, coordinated photographers and retouchers, and adapted the equipment to real inventory and client operations. Gate9 was the platform's internal production unit.

PhotoPizza originated in this work as a motorized turntable for accessible photo-360 product capture. After MEGAVISOR, I began developing its control software myself in JavaScript and Espruino and continued it as an independent open-source hardware and software project.

I also contributed product ideas for the MEGAVISOR player. It presented photo-360 sequences, spherical 3D panoramas, and virtual tours, including navigation from a panorama to an interactive view of an object shown inside it. My brother Alex focused on the cloud product and led the online-platform development team.
