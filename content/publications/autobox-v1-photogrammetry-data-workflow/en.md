AUTOBOX field scanning produced large source-image datasets that moved from museum capture to 3D production together with their capture context. The workflow covered field photography, source-data organization, color calibration, retouching, retopology, and handoff between photographers and 3D specialists.

I helped design and organize this production chain alongside the equipment and capture technology. I also participated in recruiting photographers, retouchers, and visualizers and in preparing training materials for scanning and scan processing.
