## Areas of my work

My work brings together systems engineering, applied AI, and process automation. These directions have grown through practical projects and share an engineering approach: understand the constraints, design a working system, and verify the result.

## The three directions

- **[Systems Engineering and Integration](systems-engineering/)** — a product that has to keep working as one system: request path, contracts, state, a device contour behind an explicit boundary, and the operations contour above it.
- **[Applied AI Infrastructure and Agent Delegation](applied-ai/)** — where AI execution runs, how a model or resource group is chosen, what data may leave the boundary, and how an assignment is delegated, verified, and reported.
- **[Process Automation and Verification](process-automation/)** — a repeatable operation that runs without a person watching every step, and where every step is still provable: entry conditions, per-stage checks, deduplication, recovery, and the log.
